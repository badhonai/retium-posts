every L1 launch follows the same playbook: team tokens, VC allocation, advisor shares, insider discounts, public sale at inflated price, then inevitable sell pressure when unlocks hit.

Retium replaced this entire model with something called the Safehouse. and the implications for developers building on the chain are more significant than most people realize.

here's the full breakdown:

𝟭. 𝗭𝗲𝗿𝗼 𝗔𝗹𝗹𝗼𝗰𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 — 𝗡𝗼 𝗘𝘅𝗰𝗲𝗽𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝘀

the tokenomics page states it explicitly:
• team allocation: 0
• investor allocation: 0
• advisor allocation: 0
• partner allocation: 0

nobody receives RTM before the public. nobody pays less for it. the terms are identical for everyone, regardless of when they arrive.

𝟮. 𝗧𝗵𝗲 𝗦𝗮𝗳𝗲𝗵𝗼𝘂𝘀𝗲 𝗠𝗲𝗰𝗵𝗮𝗻𝗶𝘀𝗺

RTM doesn't launch through a token sale. it enters circulation through the Safehouse — an on-chain reserve that exchanges RTM and rUSD at a fixed 1:1 rate, in both directions:

• deposit 1 rUSD → receive 1 RTM
• return 1 RTM → redeem 1 rUSD

supply only moves into circulation when matching value enters the reserve. there's no speculative launch price, no price discovery chaos, no VC cliff waiting to dump on retail.

𝟯. 𝗙𝗶𝘅𝗲𝗱 𝗦𝘂𝗽𝗽𝗹𝘆 + 𝗗𝗲𝗳𝗹𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻𝗮𝗿𝘆 𝗕𝘂𝗿𝗻

total supply is capped at 1,000,000,000 RTM with 18 decimal precision. no minting beyond genesis. no admin key, governance vote, or emergency function can increase supply.

on top of that, 1% of every transaction fee is permanently burned. this means:
• the more the network is used, the scarcer RTM becomes
• deflation comes from real transaction volume, not artificial team burns
• every token you hold represents a slightly larger share of supply over time

𝟰. 𝗪𝗵𝘆 𝗧𝗵𝗶𝘀 𝗠𝗮𝘁𝘁𝗲𝗿𝘀 𝗳𝗼𝗿 𝗗𝗲𝘃𝗲𝗹𝗼𝗽𝗲𝗿𝘀

as a developer choosing which chain to build on, the token distribution model directly affects your project's economics:

→ no VC unlock events that crash the token your dApp depends on
→ no vesting cliffs that create predictable sell pressure
→ no insider advantage that undermines community trust
→ your treasury management doesn't need to account for token allocation schedules

the Safehouse gives every builder the same starting conditions. that predictability is worth more than any grant program.

𝟱. 𝗩𝗮𝗹𝗶𝗱𝗮𝘁𝗼𝗿 𝗘𝗰𝗼𝗻𝗼𝗺𝗶𝗰𝘀 𝗪𝗶𝘁𝗵𝗼𝘂𝘁 𝗜𝗻𝗳𝗹𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻

validators on Retium are paid entirely from real transaction fees — not from newly minted tokens. there is 0% inflation.

the reward distribution per finalized block:
• Workers: 90%
• Keepers: 3%
• Suits: 0.5%
• Treasury: 0.5%
• Foundation: 5%
• Burn: 1%

every coin earned is backed by real work performed on the network. no passive income from inflation. no printing.

the Safehouse isn't just a launch mechanism. it's a statement about what a fair blockchain economy looks like.
