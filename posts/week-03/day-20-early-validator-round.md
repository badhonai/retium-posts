Early Validator Round pricing:
• Worker seat: $100
• Suit seat: $1,000

post-mainnet pricing:
• Worker seat: $1,000
• Suit seat: $10,000

that's a permanent 10× price advantage for early participants.

𝟯. 𝗹𝗼𝘄 𝗯𝗮𝗿𝗿𝗶𝗲𝗿 𝘁𝗼 𝗲𝗻𝘁𝗿𝘆

the Suit tier requires 100,000 RTM. Suits serve as finality anchors · verifying sealed blocks, coordinating finality votes, and maintaining state synchronization.

the Keeper tier requires 1,000,000 RTM. Keepers maintain PrimeMesh topology, coordinate ticks, and recompute deterministic mathematical proofs.

• 90% → Workers
• 5% → Foundation
• 3% → Keepers
• 1% → Protocol Burn (permanent deflation)
• 0.5% → Suits
• 0.5% → Treasury

the 90% Worker allocation is the key design choice. it distributes income to the broadest validator tier, incentivizing maximum participation at the most accessible entry point.

the accessible pricing isn't just an economic incentive. it's a mechanism to strengthen the network's security through broader participation.
