"if a block does not fit the PrimeMesh, it is rejected" · this single sentence explains why the testnet reset on Aug 20 behaved the way it did.

when validators disagreed under load, the network didn't fork into competing chains. it stalled. because PrimeMesh's deterministic structure means there is no competing path to choose between. a block either fits the prime-number topology or it doesn't.

on Ethereum, the same disagreement would produce two chain heads and the network would eventually pick one through its finality mechanism. on Retium, the math decides before the block can take its place · so invalid state simply never enters the mesh.

halt-and-fix is a safer failure mode than fork-and-hope. the testnet proved that.
