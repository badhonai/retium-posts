## 📋 Post — copy & paste

```text
the underrated part of priority credits is that they're optional and bounded.

+10% at Level 1, +20% at Level 2, on top of a fee that was already known before you submitted. so the worst case is a Weight 5 transaction at $0.54.

compare that to a gas auction, where "priority" is unbounded and the ceiling is whatever the busiest block will bear. you aren't bidding against strangers — you're choosing a multiplier on a fixed number.

that's a very different thing to build against. a dApp can set its own priority policy and budget it precisely, instead of reacting to a market it doesn't control.
```
