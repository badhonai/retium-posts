fees are denominated in USD and converted to $RTM through an oracle rate. so you always know the real-world cost.

no gas auctions. no bidding wars. no "your transaction failed because someone paid more."

for developers, this changes everything about UX design:

→ you can show users the exact fee before they confirm
→ you can budget infrastructure costs precisely
→ no "gas token" hacks or fee abstraction layers needed
→ subscription models, micro-payments, and pay-per-use all become viable

Priority Level 1: +10% fee for faster inclusion
Priority Level 2: +20% fee for fastest inclusion

also worth noting: 1% of every fee is permanently burned. so the supply gets more scarce with usage. deflationary by design, not by token burns from team wallets.
