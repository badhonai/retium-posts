the Safehouse model is something I haven't seen in any other L1 and it solves a problem most projects don't even acknowledge.

the typical flow is: team allocates tokens → insiders buy at a discount → public buys at launch price → insiders dump on retail. the token launch itself creates the sell pressure.

Retium skips all of that. zero team allocation. zero investor discount. zero advisory tokens. the Safehouse exchanges RTM and rUSD at 1:1, same rate for everyone, always.

Deposit 1 rUSD → receive 1 RTM
Return 1 RTM → redeem 1 rUSD

as a developer building on this, that predictability matters. I don't have to worry about a VC unlock event cratering the token my dApp depends on. I don't have to time my treasury management around vesting cliffs.

combined with the 0% inflation and the 1% fee burn, the economic model is: usage drives deflation. the more the network is used, the scarcer RTM becomes. not through artificial burns, but through actual transaction volume.

reward distribution is also worth noting — 90% goes to Workers (the most accessible validator role at 10K RTM minimum stake). that's designed to distribute income broadly instead of concentrating it among the highest-stake nodes.

this is the tokenomics model I'd want to build on. predictable costs, no insider advantage, deflationary by usage.
